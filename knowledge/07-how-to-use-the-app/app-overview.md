# App overview

**Status:** DRAFT
**Purpose:** A map of the Zeronix Intelligence application: what each screen is for and where to start, so a new team member (or an AI helping them) knows where things are.
**Sources:** the application's own documentation and screens (docs/design/SCREENS.md, docs/modules). Written from the documentation, not from a walk-through of the live screens, so check it against what you see.

## The idea in one paragraph

Zeronix holds no stock. Suppliers send prices and stock all day (mostly by WhatsApp). The app keeps each original message safe as evidence, turns it into structured prices and stock **after a person checks it**, and then lets the team search that knowledge when a customer enquires. Nothing extracted by the app becomes fact until a person confirms it.

## Two roles

- **Admin:** everything, plus users, mailboxes and settings.
- **Staff:** all the daily work; no Settings.

## The rules that shape everything

- The original message is never changed. Prices and stock come from it as **observations** (product, supplier, value, when observed, evidence).
- A wrong observation is **retracted**, never edited or deleted, so the history stays honest.
- Missing information stays **unknown** (a missing quantity, VAT, warranty, currency or part number is left blank, not guessed).

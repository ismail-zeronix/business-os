# Business model

**Status:** DRAFT
**Purpose:** How Zeronix makes money and how an order flows, so an AI plans work that fits the model.
**Sources:** the owner, zeronix.ae and biometricdevice.ae (read 2026-09-27).

## Already established

- Zeronix holds no stock. It sources from suppliers after a customer enquiry (owner).
- Supplier prices and stock change constantly, so they are recorded as dated observations.

## Ways Zeronix earns (from the websites and the owner)

- **Product supply:** IT and security hardware in the categories listed in brands-and-categories, sourced from suppliers.
- **Services:** IT support and AMC, CCTV, biometric systems, cybersecurity, networking, servers, backup, audio visual, Microsoft cloud (see service-catalogue).
- **Software solutions:** the EazyTime attendance platform (subscription plans; see software-solutions) and software and web development.
- **Lead generation:** the SEO-focused domain biometricdevice.ae brings in leads for biometric systems (see websites-and-lead-sources).

## EazyTime in one line

"Intelligent Time & Attendance for Contract & Multi-Site Workforce": a cloud-based and on-premise attendance management platform designed for UAE businesses (eazytime.app).

## Who it is for (as the site states it)

Facility management, construction, supply chain and logistics, manpower and staffing, retail chains, manufacturing, cleaning and janitorial services, landscaping, and other multi-site or contract workforces.

## How staff clock in

- Mobile app (iOS and Android) with GPS verification.
- Biometric terminals (fingerprint or face recognition).
- QR code or NFC card scanning.
- Web portal check-in.
- Kiosk tablet (PIN or Face ID).

## Main functions

- Job and activity tracking by location or project; shift scheduling and rosters; overtime and break rule automation; timesheets; leave and time-off management; reports and analytics.
- Document tracking (Emirates ID, visas, labour cards) with expiry alerts.
- Central control of ZKTeco devices.
- Fraud control: AI face recognition with fraud detection at clock-in to block proxy punches, and GPS geofencing so staff can clock in only inside the registered site.
- Offline safety: if a device is offline, every punch is stored on the ZKTeco device and syncs when it reconnects.

## Editions and hosting (as the site states it)

- **EazyTime Workforce:** the full platform and the most common choice. Mobile app and web check-in included; biometric device integration is optional.
- **EazyTime Sync:** attendance is pushed into a customer's existing HRMS without the full dashboard.
- **Cloud:** the customer gets their own branded subdomain, in the form `yourcompany.eazytime.app`.
- **On-premise:** runs on the customer's own servers or private cloud.

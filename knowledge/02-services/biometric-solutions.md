# Biometric solutions

**Status:** DRAFT
**Purpose:** What Zeronix offers for biometric attendance and access control, and how those enquiries are handled. This is the product line the biometricdevice.ae site is built to generate leads for.
**Sources:** biometricdevice.ae and zeronix.ae (read 2026-09-27).

## What is offered (as the site states it)

- **Hardware:** face recognition terminals, fingerprint and palm readers, card and RFID readers, turnstiles, speed gates and barriers. Brands named: ZKTeco, Suprema, Hikvision.
- **Delivery options:** supply only, or supply with installation.
- **Services:** free on-site survey (no deposit), installation and cabling, software configuration, staff training and handover, remote and on-site support, annual maintenance contracts.
- **Attendance software:** EazyTime, see software-solutions. The terminals it works with include ZKTeco, Suprema, Dahua and Hikvision.

## How an enquiry is handled (as the site states it)

1. The customer asks for a survey; the site says it is free with no deposit.
2. After the survey, a **fixed written quotation** is issued covering hardware, software, installation and training, with "no line items appearing later".
3. Coverage: all 7 emirates; Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah in 24 to 48 hours, Northern Emirates in 2 to 3 working days.
